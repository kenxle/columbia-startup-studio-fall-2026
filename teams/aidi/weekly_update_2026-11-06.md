# Weekly Update: AIDI (Aplovia), November 6, 2026

## Accountability: what we did

- **Build:** Added a registration system so user profiles, preferences, and progress can persist across sessions. Added My College List as the foundation for moving from college discovery into early application planning. Expanded the dataset to 50 colleges to provide broader search coverage and more institutional information. This work followed our product plan and established the foundation needed for launch.
- **Grow:** Maintained a waitlist of 17 people and identified 12 committed launch users. We did not begin a broader acquisition campaign because the product was still pre-launch and the team prioritized the core user experience first.
- **Analyze:** Synthesized three usability sessions into recurring findings and prioritized changes. The analysis identified unclear save and next-step behavior, unexplained terminology, incomplete Chinese localization, difficult detail-page navigation, and unclear post-waitlist messaging.
- **Research:** Ran three usability sessions with a college admissions counselor, a Columbia University senior, and a high school student. All three participants reached the aha moment when they found the original data-source links and said that source transparency increased trust.

## Planning: what we're doing next

- **Build:** Launch the product, add an application task checklist to My College List, expand the dataset from 50 to 100 colleges, and implement the prioritized usability fixes. These changes support both immediate launch readiness and the longer path from college discovery to application preparation.
- **Grow:** Onboard the 12 committed launch users and coordinate messaging across Chinese and international social-media accounts. This gives us an initial user group while we determine which channels can reliably reach students in both markets.
- **Analyze:** Track account creation, activation, and the movement from the waitlist into product use after launch. This will show where users stop before reaching the product's value.
- **Research:** Run follow-up usability sessions after the highest-priority fixes are implemented. This will test whether users can understand the terminology, complete and save their preferences, navigate institution details, and understand the post-waitlist state without assistance.

## Issues

- Coordinating content and messaging across Chinese and international social-media accounts.
- Ensuring that users in mainland China can reliably access the deployed product and its required third-party services.
