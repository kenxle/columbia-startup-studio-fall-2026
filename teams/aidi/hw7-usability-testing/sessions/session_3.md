### Task-by-task observations

#### Task 1: Explore the public pages and join the waitlist

- **What they did:** Explored the homepage and landing pages and proceeded through the public product entry points without assistance.
- **What they said out loud:** The interface looked clean and professional, which made the product feel trustworthy, although some interactions were not immediately clear.
- **Completed?** Yes

#### Task 2: Enter preferences, search, and filter colleges

- **What they did:** Entered background information and preferences and reviewed the available search and filter choices. They paused at unfamiliar terms and after completing the fields to look for a save or next-step action.
- **What they said out loud:** Some content remained in English while using the Chinese interface. Specialized terms needed explanations in both Chinese and English, and it was unclear whether the entered information had been saved.
- **Completed?** With trouble

#### Task 3 (aha moment): Review a college detail page and verify its sources

- **What they did:** Opened a college detail page, scrolled through it to locate specific sections, and found the original data-source links.
- **What they said out loud:** The source links increased confidence in the information, but the long detail page needed a faster way to move between sections.
- **Completed?** With trouble

**Hesitations:** Paused at unfamiliar terminology, after completing the preference fields, and while searching the long detail page for specific information.

**Wrong clicks:** None observed.

**Questions out loud:** Asked what specialized terms meant and whether the preference information had been saved.

**Dropped tasks:** None.

**Workarounds:** Scrolled through the detail page because there was no quick section navigation.

**Did they reach the aha moment?** Yes. They reached it when they found the original data-source links and said that showing the sources increased the product's credibility.

### Post-session answers

1. **Overall impression:** The interface was clean, professional, and trustworthy.
2. **Most confusing or difficult part:** Unexplained terminology, English content in the Chinese interface, unclear save and next-step behavior, and navigation within the detail page.
3. **Most important improvement:** Explain specialized terms bilingually and make the next action and saved state clear.

### Quick insight

The biggest thing this session showed us was that a clean and credible presentation was not enough when key interactions and terminology remained unclear.
