# Restroom Ready weekly update

**Team:** Five Girls  
**Date:** November 6, 2026

**What we built and why**

Since the midterm demo, we simplified the iPhone restroom cards, grouped ratings and public notes under Rate and Comment, and added a moderator dashboard for new restroom requests and inappropriate comments. We are distributing our [public TestFlight link](https://testflight.apple.com/join/PxWSpdJ8) and expect more users in the coming days.

We completed [three usability sessions](hw8-usability-testing/README.md). Basic map navigation worked, and two testers successfully saved and reopened places. Issues included an unclear empty filter result, missing hours on an approved submission, and disorganized expanded condition reports. The third tester needed guidance to understand Share when reporting an entry-permission update, then could not see the change where expected. This highlighted unclear contribution actions and results.

**What we are building next and why**

We will organize reported issues, clarify submission buttons and show where contributed information appears or awaits review. We will improve filter empty states and hours entry, including investigating editable hours suggestions from place search. Follow-up testing will check these changes, public-note submission and saving through a new sign-in. We also plan to bring requests and problem reports together for moderators. We are refining the legal disclaimers about app data to make its sources, limitations and changing conditions clearer.

**Current user count**

10 TestFlight downloads. We have also completed three usability sessions.

**Biggest blocker**

Our online visibility and user growth have not met our HW6 demand forecast. We are sharing the TestFlight link more widely and comparing outreach channels while using the usability findings to prioritize improvements.
