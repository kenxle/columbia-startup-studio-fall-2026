# HW8: Usability testing (Gable)

Sessions over Zoom, Nov 6, recorded with consent. We tested the staging MVP: https://gable-git-staging-gable2.vercel.app

## Session 1 (Sam)
**Tester:** [MSAI graduate student]

**What we observed**
- The AI search was the aha moment: she typed her request right away and said "wowwwww this is great!!!!"
- She clicked "Summer" in the header and ended up on a landing page instead of searching.
- The commute lines on listings ("20 min to Times Square") and "Replies in ~3h" confused her.
- She tried to sign in before having an account and asked if a Columbia email works.
- She didn't realize the listings come from Columbia students.
- She liked the favorites map but expected more listings per neighborhood.

**What we're changing, and why**
- Simplify the header links in the search flow, so people don't get pulled out of the product.
- Replace the confusing card lines with plain info, or drop them.
- Make "Create account" the obvious option for new users.
- Show "Listed by a Columbia student" on each listing, since that's our main trust promise.
