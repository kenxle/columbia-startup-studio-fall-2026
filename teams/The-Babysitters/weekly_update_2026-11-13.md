# The Babysitters — Weekly update, November 13

## Accountability

- **Build.** Last week we planned to complete a challenge from entry through judging and results. We implemented the challenge flow, a skills library and supporting runner changes, and tested them locally. We have not released the complete flow. We also fixed the live Tycoon game-opening failure and verified that a new game could open.

- **Grow.** We do not have returning users beyond the people we have interviewed directly.

- **Analyze.** Testing exposed problems with submissions and reports in the challenge implementation. Feedback on the existing site also showed that progress, status and returned results were difficult to follow.

- **Research.** We spoke with Jonah, a heavy Claude user; Collin, who uses Claude Code for quantitative trading; and a SIPA student recruited by Mansi. Each conversation lasted about 10 minutes. The SIPA participant did not proceed to using the site; her feedback concerned the product’s usefulness. We also ran separate agent simulations to examine the visit flow.

## Planning

- **Build.** Make the challenge-to-skill flow work end to end: an agent enters, submits work, receives a judgment, and the winning approach becomes a skill another user can obtain and use. Simplify the visit experience into one page with clear status, results and next steps.

- **Grow.** Invite the people we interviewed to try the completed flow and see whether they return without a guided session.

- **Analyze.** Check whether users can start a visit, understand what the agent is doing, find its result and use the returned skill. Track where they need help.

- **Research.** Test the completed flow with new users, including strangers, and observe whether the skills help with their own work.

## Issues

- The challenge and skills implementation still needs a coordinated release of the website and runner.

- The existing visit interface does not consistently explain progress or when results are ready.

- We have not yet completed three hands-on usability sessions; Mansi’s conversation stopped before website use.
