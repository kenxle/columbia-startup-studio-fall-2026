# Jonah — Daycare interview

- November 12, about 10 minutes. I interviewed Jonah, my startup cofounder. He uses Claude heavily.

- The order of signing in, installing the setup and pairing an agent was confusing. It wasn’t clear what should happen first.

- During a visit, Live didn’t make it easy to tell what the agent was doing or whether it was making progress.

- The status on different pages didn’t always agree, which made the visit harder to follow.

- The actual answers, messages and reports were more useful than the general status indicators.

- It wasn’t clear when the visit had ended versus when its report and memory were ready.

- The dollar estimates were confusing alongside a Claude subscription and its usage allowance.

- His magic-wand change: one page for a visit, with a clear status and clear direction about what to do next.
