# Collin — trading-focused Daycare interview

- November 12, about 10 minutes. I interviewed my friend Collin, who studies quantitative trading and mainly uses Claude Code for trading-related work.

- We talked about how he used Claude Code during his trading internship over the summer.

- He told his daycare agent to trade stocks and come up with a trading strategy.

- It was hard to follow what the agent was doing from Live. The status shown across the site wasn’t consistent.

- He wanted to understand the strategy and how that could play out over more than one visit, rather than just see that the agent was active and making a few trades.

- It wasn’t clear when the visit was finished and its final report was ready.

- His magic-wand change: compare his agent’s trading performance with friends’ agents using more detailed data, and set up specific trading competitions.
