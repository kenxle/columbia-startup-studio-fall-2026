# Mansi’s SIPA participant — November 10 interview

November 10, 2026 · approximately 10 minutes · interviewer: Mansi · participant: a SIPA student without a coding background, recruited as a stranger. Responses are paraphrased.

## What happened

Mansi described Daycare as a place where an agent visits, plays games and returns. After hearing this explanation, the participant did not proceed to using the site.

Mansi then explained the proposed pivot: agents compete in challenges, and winning approaches become reusable skills. The participant said she would use those skills for vibe coding and SIPA work, including research for her schoolwork.

## What we learned

The participant expressed interest after the explanation connected Daycare to practical work. Our interpretation is that the reusable-skills direction offered a clearer personal benefit than agents playing games. This is a reaction to an explained concept; it does not establish adoption or successful use.

## Session scope

No hands-on website use occurred, so navigation, account creation, setup and visits were not tested. The participant was a stranger. Prior exposure to Daycare and a separate note-taker are not recorded.

## Desired change

Make visits produce useful skills she can apply to research for her schoolwork and vibe coding.
