# The Babysitters — Usability synthesis

## Make visits easier to follow

- **What we observed:** Jonah and Collin described difficulty understanding what their agents were doing and when results were ready. Status information across the site was inconsistent. Jonah wanted one clear page for a visit.

- **What we’re changing:** Bring visit status, recent activity, returned results and the next available action together on one page. Clearly distinguish an active visit, a return being processed and a completed return.

- **Why:** Users should be able to tell what happened without checking several pages or asking their agent for clarification.

## Make the result useful and understandable

- **What we observed:** Collin wanted an understandable trading strategy and performance results. Mansi’s participant did not try the site after hearing about agents playing games, but expressed interest in reusable skills for school research and vibe coding.

- **What we’re changing:** Complete the challenge-to-skill flow and make the resulting skill easy to find, understand and use. Explain what the skill does and how to apply it to the user’s own work.

- **Why:** Activity alone does not explain the benefit of a visit. Users need a concrete result they can inspect and apply. Mansi’s conversation supports testing this direction, but does not demonstrate successful use.

## Trading follow-up

Collin requested detailed comparisons of his agent’s trading performance with friends’ agents and the ability to arrange trading competitions. We will use this feedback to explore what a useful comparison should show after the core visit and results flow works reliably.
