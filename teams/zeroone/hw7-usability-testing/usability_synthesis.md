# CallIt usability synthesis

On November 6, three first-time users tried CallIt. We watched them join a group, suggest a prediction, and answer another prediction. In CallIt, a member's suggestion becomes an open call when a group manager publishes it.

## 1. Show what happens after a suggestion is sent

**What we observed.** All three participants showed some uncertainty about publication. P1 read the waiting message and worked out that a manager had to publish the suggestion. P2 described who could see it under different conditions but did not clearly identify its current state. P3 needed the moderator to explain the waiting state.

**What we changed.** The submission message says that a manager must publish the suggestion, explains who can see it, and gives the author a way to reopen it.

**Why.** Participants could submit a suggestion and still be unsure whether the group could see it or who needed to act next.

## 2. Make New call easier to find

**What we observed.** P1 opened an existing call when asked to create one and continued after the moderator clarified the task. P2 created a call without help and later said that Answer was easier to spot than New call.

**What we changed.** We made New call visible in the phone navigation and more prominent on the group page.

**Why.** A visible, labeled action helps users find where to create a question. P1's detour may also reflect the way we worded the task.

## 3. Mark required fields before submission

**What we observed.** P3 needed help finding the required group name and the required description for a custom suggestion. P2 completed custom creation without help. P1 used a sports template.

**What we changed.** We marked required fields, kept the group description optional, and explained what to enter in What counts.

**Why.** P3's experience shows where clearer labels and guidance can help. The other sessions covered different forms or showed different results.
